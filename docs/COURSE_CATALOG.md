# Course catalog

Implementation: `src/core/arcade.c and src/core/menu.c`.

Course selection reads disc-backed lists and localized text. Course identifiers connect menu selections to the world, collision data and driving lines loaded for a race. A listed course is not evidence that all of its modes are verified.

## Evidence and limitations

The [development record](notes/COURSE_CATALOG.md) retains addresses, experiments,
test results and outstanding limitations. Older entries describe the state
at their recorded date and may be superseded. Consult [Fidelity](FIDELITY.md)
for per-item grades; implementation coverage is not full-game certification.
